That historical rescan reused source-bound November 12 Gradle snapshots; it did not re-resolve dependencies. The current worklist incorporates later fresh candidate snapshots with unchanged fingerprints and retains each package/version, affected and fixed ranges, scope, group and source identity.

These are advisory-document investigations, not demonstrations of exploitability or completed independent dispositions. Every occurrence remains **Further investigation**, failing and unaccepted. The mod JARs do not bundle these dependencies, but game, launcher, loader, build or other-mod paths may still be relevant. Reported upstream fixed events are package- and release-line specific; check the exact coordinate and compatibility before any remediation.
